## Reading a state file

Each table contains the exact advertised title, employer, location, official link, checked date, and corresponding role ID from [suitable_roles.md](../roles/suitable_roles.md). Read the fit notes below the table before applying. Rankings reflect the resume, not confirmed employer eligibility.

- **Verified public listing / appears open:** the employer page or employer-branded recruiting system displayed the role, relevant location and an application route without a closure notice. This confirms the public listing only; available headcount was not confirmed with a recruiter.
- **Unverified:** direct access, freshness or current application status could not be established. Search snippets and older announcements are leads, not confirmed vacancies.
- **High / Medium fit:** assessment of documented experience. Destination expertise, CRM tools, languages, travel preference and notice period remain partly unknown.
- Checked dates are research dates, not posting dates. Retrieved pages may be cached. Applications were not submitted and form submission was not tested.
- A branch office or company headquarters alone is insufficient to assign a vacancy to a state. Only locations stated for the role are included. Role-specific remote eligibility is not inferred from general company benefits.
