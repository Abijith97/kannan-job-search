# Big Four employers

The website checks relevant marketing, communications, client programmes, research, sales support and coordination vacancies across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Telangana and Maharashtra. A firm name or an Analyst/Associate title alone does not establish eligibility.

| Company | Official careers | Automated coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EY | [India careers](https://careers.ey.com/ey/search/?optionsFacetsDD_country=IN) | EY India and GDS career search pages and exact vacancy descriptions. |
| Deloitte | [US-India jobs](https://usijobs.deloitte.com/en_US/careersUSI/SearchJobs) | USI career search pages and exact vacancy descriptions. |
| PwC | [India careers](https://www.pwc.in/careers.html) | Public Workday vacancy search and job-detail responses, filtered to supported locations. |
| KPMG | [India careers](https://kpmg.com/in/en/careers.html) | Public KGS and KDN India Oracle vacancy searches and job-detail responses. The separate KPMG India TalentRecruit board is available through the official careers page; automated coverage does not include that board. |

Open the [website](https://kannan-job-search.abijith97-jobs.workers.dev) and sign in. Shortlisted openings appear in the same jobs table as every other employer. Use the existing search box to search a company name, role or location. There is no separate employer section or company filter.

Every new shortlisted job must pass the same full-description resume assessment and score at least 65/100 with documented work-experience evidence. Required qualifications, specialist skills and seniority still apply. Active Big Four postings may have older publication dates; their original dates remain visible. Missing dates are never guessed.

The nightly collector checks these employers directly without extra OpenAI web-search calls. It can stage up to six new verified candidates per firm; the shared assessment batch remains capped at 30 vacancies across all sources. Coverage is bounded and not exhaustive. Source failures preserve earlier listings; explicitly closed vacancies are hidden. Current matches and checks are in the website and [snapshot](../data/job-snapshot.json).
