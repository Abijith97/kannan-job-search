A SIMPLE GUIDE

How to use this website

Sign in with your shared passphrase, then use these steps to find a role, prepare your resume and apply.

  1. Find suitable jobs

    Filter by Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Telangana or Maharashtra. Search by role, company or city, and sort by publication date. Listings without a date appear last.

  2. Explore profile matches

    Select Show → Profile matches to see jobs assessed against your saved resume. Open Why it fits to read the match explanation, score and requirements to confirm.

  3. Check newly discovered jobs

    The shortlist updates automatically around 2 a.m. IST. Newly discovered listings display New for seven days. Select Show → New listings to find them quickly.

  4. Create a custom resume

    Click Create custom resume. The website uses the job description to select relevant experience from your saved resume. If it cannot read the listing, paste the description into the form.

  5. Review and edit the download

    Your resume downloads as an editable Word (.docx) file. Review it, make changes in Word and save the finished version. A PDF download is also available.

  6. Apply through the employer

    Use View opening or Apply on employer site to submit your application and upload the finished resume. The website does not submit applications automatically.

  7. Save your progress for later

    Use Skip for unwanted roles and Restore to reconsider them. View resume reopens a prepared resume for downloading again without regeneration. Your choices and saved resumes remain available across devices.
