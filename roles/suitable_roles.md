## Resume improvements before applying

1. **Substantiate the 12% revenue claim:** identify the period, whether it means total company or vertical revenue, absolute amount if disclosable, and individual versus shared attribution. Keep the claim only if supportable.
2. **Make partnership outcomes concrete:** distinguish signed, activated and revenue-producing partners; add partner category, geography, lead flow and contribution per period where known.
3. **Keep conversion denominators:** 5/15 is 33.3%; 10/17 is 58.8%. These are small samples, so avoid presenting them as proven performance at scale. The stated social growth percentages are arithmetically consistent: approximately 86% and 188%.
4. **Replace vague efficiency language:** give before/after call duration, qualified leads, or conversions for the lead-qualification change. More calls or shorter calls alone do not establish better sales outcomes.
5. **Name tools actually used:** CRM, automation and analytics platforms, plus a concrete example of each. Do not insert Salesforce, Zoho, SQL or advanced Power BI expertise solely because a vacancy asks for them.
6. **Clarify destination and process experience:** which countries, courses, admissions stages and documentation tasks he handled professionally. Add languages and proficiency levels only after confirmation.
7. **Keep official job titles:** under Research Analyst, use a truthful explanatory line about education market research, sales and operations to make the commercial scope clear. Resolve the internship/full-time overlap in August 2023 with exact dates if needed.
8. **Tailor two versions:** a partnerships/BD resume emphasizing 13 partners and commercial outcomes; an admissions resume emphasizing counselling and enrolment conversions. Maintain the original source until details are confirmed.
