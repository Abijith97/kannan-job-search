## Verification standard

**Verified listing / appears open** means the employer's career page or employer-branded recruiting system displayed the specific role, an Indian location and an application route when checked, with no closure notice. This verifies the public listing, not that a recruiter has confirmed available headcount. Some retrieved pages can be cached. No applications were submitted, and submission success was not tested.

**Unverified** means live detail or application status could not be established. Search snippets and older hiring announcements alone do not count as verified current vacancies. Dates below are check dates, not publication dates.
